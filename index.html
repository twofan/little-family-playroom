Little Family Playroom

Numbers, music and garden mazes

Four gentle games with a friendly family and their playful dog. Play offline, at your own pace, with no ads, purchases or account.

Help for parents

Tap the house to return to the four games. Hold the gear for 2.5 seconds to open the parent guide, switch English/Mandarin number speech or reset the current game.

  • Calculator: tap numbers and operations, or add and take away biscuits with Mum and Dad. The next number waits until the current speech finishes. Clear and undo can interrupt.
  • Challenges: tap the seedling to choose among five types of number puzzles. Tap the picnic cloth for a quantity demonstration. There is no timer or penalty.
  • Music: tap a player to start; tap musicians to let them rest or join. The score changes the song. Finger mode advances the melody one tap at a time.
  • Maze: tap a path to walk to the next junction. Portraits select family gardens; footprints show a hint. Tap Hotdog three times to discover his garden.

Contact support

Send a support request on GitHub. Tell us your app version, device model and what happened. GitHub requests are public: please do not include your child's name, photos, school, location or other private information. A GitHub account is needed to post.

No sound?

On your playing device, check the volume and audio output, then return home and open the game again. Music starts only when you tap a musician. The app never uses a microphone.

亲子小乐园 · 家长帮助

四款离线小游戏：野餐计算器、数字挑战、家庭小乐队和花园迷宫。无广告、内购或账号。

点房子回到主页；长按齿轮 2.5 秒打开家长指南，切换中文/英文数字语音，或重置当前游戏。计算器会等当前语音读完再接受下一次数字输入，清零与撤销可随时操作。

数字挑战的小苗选择五类题目，点餐布看数量演示。音乐里点乐手加入或休息，乐谱切换曲子，手指模式每次点按弹一个旋律音。迷宫点路走到下个岔口，脚印给提示，点 Hotdog 三次发现隐藏花园。

通过 GitHub 联系支持：请提供应用版本、设备型号与问题描述。反馈公开可见，请勿上传孩子的姓名、照片、学校、位置或其他私人信息。发布反馈需要 GitHub 账号。

没有声音时，请在游玩设备上检查音量与输出设备，然后回主页重新进入游戏。音乐需点乐手才开始；应用不使用麦克风。
