Little Family Playroom

Numbers, music and a family garden

Gentle offline games with a friendly family and their playful dog. No ads, purchases or account.

Help for parents · current versions

These instructions cover version 1.20, currently available through TestFlight with six activities. App Store 1.11 has five activities and does not include Bubble Bath or the first-time parent welcome. Tap the house to return home. Tap the gear to open parent settings, then Done to return; versions up to 1.11 use a 2.5-second hold. The first-time welcome introduces the family project and optional Guided Access, which keeps the device in one app. Its setup and ending instructions can be reopened in settings. Top arrows change the current number, level, song or character; tap the current picture or number to see all choices. Language is available at home, in the chooser and in the parent guide.

  • Calculator: add or subtract with up to 10 digits per number. Each accepted input reads the complete number before another mathematical input is accepted. Clear, Undo and navigation can interrupt. Mum adds biscuits; Dad takes them away.
  • Challenges: choose among five puzzle types. Tap the picnic table for a quantity demonstration; version 1.10 and later also demonstrate after a wrong choice. No timer or penalty; the child still chooses the answer.
  • Music: tap a player to start, then tap musicians to rest or join. Version 1.8 and later show sleeping artwork for resting instruments. In version 1.13 and later, putting every musician to sleep also pauses the tune until one wakes. Version 1.20 adds acoustic instruments and balances the band above quieter scene music. The chooser includes songs, casts and a finger mode that advances the melody one tap at a time.
  • Garden: tap an object; the character walks to it and collects it into the basket. Bring all three home. Tap Hotdog once for digging, splashing and sock-fetching. In version 1.11 and later, tap teddy or the basket at the finished family picnic to make the bear hide and peek, then return to rest.
  • Hotdog Bubble Bath (1.19+): tap the dog to wash a dirty spot, tap the soap for a foam hat, then tap the duck to shake off foam. When he is clean, a towel lets him finish with a cuddle. Replay is optional.
  • Block Theatre: choose a number and watch its original animated story. Version 1.7 and later include 0–20. Tap a paused stage to continue; the replay arrow restarts the chosen story.

Version 1.4 and later start fresh whenever you enter a game or return after putting the app in the background. Your language choice stays on the device; version 1.12 also remembers completion or skipping of the parent welcome.

If you have App Store version 1.0

The older version has four activities, without Block Theatre, and preserves local progress. The seedling opens challenge types, the score changes music, and maze paths lead to the next junction. Tap Hotdog three times to reveal his garden. These controls differ from newer TestFlight versions.

Contact support

Send a support request on GitHub. Tell us your app version, device model and what happened. Requests are public: do not include your child's name, photos, school, location or other private information. A GitHub account is needed to post.

No sound?

On your playing device, check the volume and audio output, then return home and open the game again. Music starts when you tap a musician. The app never uses a microphone.

亲子小乐园 · 家长帮助

离线游玩，无广告、内购或账号。以下说明适用于 1.20 版本，目前可通过 TestFlight 游玩，共六款游戏。App Store 1.11 有五款游戏，不包含泡泡浴或首次家长指南。点房子回主页；点齿轮打开家长设置，点「完成」返回；1.11 及以前版本使用长按 2.5 秒。首次指南介绍家庭小作品，并推荐可选的「引导式访问」，减少误退出 App；设置内可以再次查看设置与结束步骤。顶栏箭头切换数字、关卡、曲子或人物；点中间的数字或图片查看全部选择。语言选择在主页、选择板和家长指南中。

  • 计算器：每个数最多 10 位，每次输入都读出完整数字；读完再接受数学操作，清零、撤销和导航可随时打断。妈妈添饼干，爸爸拿走。
  • 数字挑战：五类题目，点餐桌看数量演示；1.10 起答错时也会演示，仍由孩子自己选答案，不限时、不扣分。
  • 家庭小乐队：点乐手开始，再点让其休息或加入。1.8 起，休息的乐手会睡着。1.13 起全部乐手睡着后，旋律也暂停等待；1.20 加入真实乐器采样，并让乐队音量区别于较轻的场景配乐。选择板可以换曲、换阵容；手指模式每次点按弹下一个旋律音。
  • 花园寻宝：点想要的物品，角色会走去、捡起并收进篮子，找齐三个后带回家。点一下 Hotdog 进入挖骨头、踩水找球和叼袜子的花园。1.11 起，家人的野餐结束后可点小熊或篮子，看它躲藏、探头，再自己安静下来。
  • Hotdog 泡泡浴（1.19 起）：点小狗让海绵洗掉脏污，点肥皂添泡沫帽子，再点鸭子甩泡泡。洗干净后用毛巾结束，是否重玩由孩子选择。
  • 积木小剧场：选一个数字观看原创动画小故事，1.7 起包含 0～20。暂停时点舞台继续，重播箭头从头播放当前故事。

1.4 及以后版本每次进入游戏或从后台回来都重新开始，保留语言选择；1.12 起还在本机记录是否完成或跳过首次家长指南。

App Store 1.0 用户

旧版有四款游戏，没有积木小剧场，并保存本机进度。小苗选择挑战类型，乐谱换曲，迷宫点路走到下个岔口，点 Hotdog 三次发现隐藏花园。操作与较新的 TestFlight 版本不同。

通过 GitHub 联系支持：请提供应用版本、设备型号与问题描述。反馈公开可见，请勿上传孩子的姓名、照片、学校、位置或其他私人信息。发布反馈需要 GitHub 账号。

没有声音时，请在游玩设备上检查音量与输出设备，然后回主页重新进入游戏。音乐需点乐手才开始；应用不使用麦克风。
