Privacy policy

Little Family Playroom / 亲子小乐园
Updated October 3, 2026

The app

We do not collect, transmit, sell or share personal information through this app. Gameplay works offline. There are no accounts, advertising, analytics, tracking, in-app purchases or third-party network SDKs.

The app does not request access to your microphone, camera, photo library, contacts or location. Number speech is bundled; music is synthesized locally or bundled. It does not record you or send gameplay to an AI service.

Data on your device

In version 1.4 and later, gameplay stays in memory and restarts on each game entry or actual background return. Language persists locally in UserDefaults; version 1.12 also remembers whether the first-time parent welcome was completed or skipped. Legacy local progress saves are removed. Earlier versions, including 1.0, stored game choices and progress locally. Resetting a game clears that game's state; deleting the app removes its local data. Device backups may include app data under your Apple settings. We have no server copy of your progress.

Parents and support

External privacy/support links are in the parent area and require an adult reading check. If a parent contacts us, we receive the information they choose to send and use it to respond and resolve the issue. Do not send children's personal information. Contact the developer through GitHub; posts there are public and subject to GitHub's terms/privacy practices.

Optional adult support in version 1.22 and later

All games remain free. Parent settings may offer an optional external Ko-fi support page for an eligible United States App Store storefront, after an adult reading check. No payment unlocks content or changes the games. The app temporarily reads Apple’s storefront country code while parent settings are active; it does not save or send that code to us or to Ko-fi. Unknown and non-US storefronts do not receive this payment link.

The app opens the plain external page without identifiers or gameplay data. Ko-fi and its payment providers process any information a parent chooses to provide under their own terms. We may receive the payment details and messages those services make available to the creator. No transaction information or supporter status returns to or is stored by the game. Do not provide children’s personal information. There is no embedded checkout, payment SDK or payment link on this common support/privacy website.

Apple and this website

Apple manages App Store downloads and may collect diagnostics according to your device settings and Apple's privacy policy. This support website is hosted by GitHub Pages, which may process technical connection information such as IP addresses under GitHub's privacy statement. We add no tracking scripts or cookies to this site.

Changes

Any change to these practices will be described here with an updated effective date. Material changes to the app's practices will also be reflected in its parent guide and App Store privacy information.

隐私政策

更新日期：2026 年 10 月 3 日。适用于「亲子小乐园」。

我们不通过应用收集、发送、出售或共享个人信息。游戏离线运行，无账号、广告、分析、追踪、内购或第三方网络 SDK，不申请麦克风、相机、照片、通讯录或位置权限。数字语音预先内置，音乐在本机生成或内置；应用不录音，也不将游玩信息发送给 AI 服务。

1.4 及以后版本的游戏状态仅在内存中，每次进入游戏或从后台回来都会重新开始。语言选择保存在本机 UserDefaults；1.12 起还会记录是否已完成或跳过首次家长指南。旧版进度存档会被清除。包括 1.0 在内的较早版本曾在本机保存游戏选择与进度。重置会清除当前游戏状态，删除应用会移除本地数据。设备备份可能依据 Apple 设置包含应用数据；我们没有服务器上的进度副本。

隐私与支持链接位于家长区域，离开应用需要成人阅读验证。家长主动联系支持时，我们只接收其自愿发送的内容，用于回复与解决问题。请勿发送孩子的个人信息。GitHub 支持反馈公开可见，适用 GitHub 的条款与隐私规则。

1.22 及以后版本中，所有游戏仍免费。美国 App Store 地区的家长设置可提供自愿支持入口，通过成人阅读验证后才打开外部 Ko-fi 页面；付款不解锁内容、不改变游戏。设置打开时仅临时读取 Apple 商店地区，不保存，也不发送给我们或 Ko-fi；其他地区或未知地区不显示此付款链接。应用不在链接中附加标识或游玩数据。

家长自愿提供的信息由 Ko-fi 及支付服务商依照各自条款处理；我们可能收到这些服务向创作者提供的付款信息和留言。交易信息及支持者状态不会返回游戏或保存在游戏中。请勿提供孩子的个人信息。无内嵌收款页面或支付 SDK，本站通用帮助与隐私页不提供付款链接。

Apple 管理商店下载，并可能依据设备设置收集诊断信息。本站由 GitHub Pages 托管，托管方可能依据其隐私声明处理 IP 地址等技术连接信息。本站没有额外追踪脚本或 Cookie。政策改变时会更新生效日期，应用数据行为的重大变化也会反映在家长指南和商店隐私说明中。
